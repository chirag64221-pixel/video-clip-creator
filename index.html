Our Story

A Craft Passed
Thread by Thread.

Dhaka fabric is woven on wooden handlooms found across the hill villages of Nepal, its diamond and zigzag motifs thrown entirely by hand — no two panels ever identical. What began as ceremonial cloth for the Dhaka topi has, in our hands, become a language of modern luxury.

Our atelier sits on Bir Singh Marga in the heart of Kathmandu, where master weavers and a new generation of designers work side by side — honouring tradition while cutting silhouettes for a global wardrobe.
